Flacara · explorations · round 02

Accent & heroes

Two questions in one sheet. §1 answers "can the navy go?" — it can, but not by using the ramp itself. §2 gives each page its own hero rather than the shared pill-plus-headline block clinicians and patients currently share.

§1 — Replacing the navy

The ramp cannot become the UI accent: §2.6 fences it to four places, and a blue-to-red gradient beside a lesion photograph reads as a severity scale, a claim the consumer device is not cleared to make. What is available is a single flat accent taken from a point on the ramp and darkened until it clears the 7:1 the navy set. One accent, new hue, every rule intact.

§2 — A hero per page

Home already has one: the lockup on the lit ground with the outline tracing in. It is doing its job and I would not replace it. Clinicians and patients share a pill, a headline and a paragraph: a placeholder, not a hero. These are drafted at the real type scale against the real stylesheet.

Home · alternate — Split light

Dir. H2 · optional

Repeatable light.

Cross-polarized and unpolarized, the same two exposures every visit, so the second picture can be compared to the first.

The ramp as an aperture rather than a bar: still the mark's gradient, still §2.6's first permitted place. Only worth building if the outline trace ever gets cut. The current hero is better.

Patients · P1 — One spot, two dates

recommended

Find it again.

A clear picture of a spot on your skin, and another one later, so you can see whether anything changed. It does not tell you what it is.

Shows the product's whole promise in one glance without asserting a result. Two dates, identical framing, no verdict. Needs a real second photograph before it ships: the same file twice is a placeholder and would be dishonest live.

Patients · P2 — Plain sentence

alternate

A record, not a verdict.

Flacara takes a clear picture of a spot on your skin. Nothing more, and nothing you have to interpret alone.

No imagery above the fold at all. The safest hero to put in front of someone who is frightened, and the one that most needs legal to read the line first.
