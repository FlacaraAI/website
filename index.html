Why it matters

Three doubts, answered before you ask them.

Lighting

Not "probably the lighting."

The housing supplies its own polarized and unpolarized light and blocks the room's. A colour difference between two dates means a difference in the skin — never a difference in the bulb overhead.

Resolution

Clinically indistinguishable.

Flacara resolves detail down to ≈16 µm — more than six times finer than the unaided eye, and clinically indistinguishable from a purpose-built DermLite DL4 on the same target.

Tracking

Finds the same spot again.

Point the housing at a spot you've photographed before and the app registers the new frame against the old one — so the photo you take today can be laid over the one from six months ago and mean something.
